Simple portion rules

Rice: usually 130–150 g cooked per main meal. Curd: 150–200 g. Chicken: 130–150 g cooked. Soya: about 45–50 g dry chunks. Paneer day: around 120 g.

Keep curd rice. A useful cutting portion is ~150 g cooked rice + 200 g curd + cucumber/carrot, paired with eggs or another protein source.

8-week progression

Build consistency first, then gradually make the same weights harder.

Measurement routine

Weight 3–4 mornings/week, waist once/week at the belly button, and photos on Day 1, 28 and 56 under similar lighting.

Adjustment rule

If both your 14-day weight trend and waist are flat, reduce intake by ~100–150 kcal/day. If you are losing very quickly and strength/energy drop, eat a little more.

General fitness guidance, not medical advice. Stop if an exercise causes sharp pain. Visible abs depend on body-fat level and genetics, so eight weeks can produce a meaningful change but cannot guarantee a six-pack.
